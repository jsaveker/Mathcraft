# Play Mathcraft

[Play in your browser](https://saveker.org/mathcraft/) · [Back to Mathcraft](../README.md)

Mathcraft has two modes: maths expeditions that unlock as you explore, and five creative worlds available immediately. Choose an explorer on the welcome screen to keep each child's progress and creations separate.

## Start an adventure

Choose **Let's play**. Each expedition has five jobs. Press **G** to travel to the current job, then **E** to gather or inspect its supplies. Press **E** again to work out what the project needs. A correct answer changes the world and earns three building blocks.

Complete all five jobs to activate the onward portal and unlock the next expedition. Replaying gives you fresh puzzles. Completed jobs, gathered supplies and questions already inspected survive reloads.

| Expedition    | Projects                                                                                                                  |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Meadow Isles  | Gather timber, finish a walkable bridge, feed the flock, plant a garden, power the portal                                 |
| Crystal Peaks | Melt an ice barrier, load a moving minecart, raise the summit lift, turn light prisms, light rescue beacons               |
| Sunset Sands  | Uncover a dinosaur fossil, pump a reservoir, restore a sun mosaic, lift a counterweighted treasure door, send a sand ship |
| Cloud Harbour | Start wind turbines, release a balloon, deliver post by glider, ring sky chimes, launch an airship                        |
| Moonbase Nova | Unfold solar panels, deploy a six-wheeled rover, inflate a habitat, transmit through a space antenna, launch a rocket     |

## Set the maths level

Open **Parent settings** using the gear button on the welcome screen. Set a maximum of **10, 20 or 100**, and choose addition, subtraction or a mixture. The default maximum is 100, with a gentler starting point up to 20.

Addition and subtraction progress independently through six difficulty steps. Three first-try answers without hints move the practised skill up one step. Two questions needing help ease it back one step. Parent settings show the current level of each skill and let you choose a starting point.

Both operands and results stay inside the selected range. Subtraction never produces negative answers. Hints explain counting on, counting back, or splitting tens and ones, with dots grouped into rows of ten. Two missed attempts reveal the hint automatically. Answering speed is never measured, and mistakes do not cost lives.

Changes to the number settings apply to the next adventure. An in-progress session keeps its questions until it ends or is restarted. An inspected question, its attempts and hint use stay fixed when you close the dialog or reload.

## Explore creative worlds

Choose **My build worlds** on the welcome screen, or press **V** during an adventure. Choose Grassland Valley, Moon Frontier, Cloud Kingdom, Desert Horizons or Crystal Highlands. All five are available regardless of maths progress.

Each creative landscape is roughly 256 blocks across, with a height limit of 80 and space for 8,000 player-built blocks. Each world and explorer has a separate allowance and saved layout. The original village and its buildings remain in Grassland Valley.

Press **G** for fast travel. Each world has a home camp and four outer sites with clear 36 × 36 foundations. You can also build throughout the surrounding landscape. Arrival places you above any buildings at the destination. The minimap shows your coordinates.

Press **E** or **I** to open the 36-block library. Choose a material to replace the currently selected hotbar slot. Your nine favourites are saved for your explorer. Glass and ice are transparent; glowstone and sea lanterns glow. Water cubes stay where you place them.

### Build with tools and blueprints

Press **T** to choose single blocks, lines, walls, floors, or a cottage, castle, tower or rocket blueprint. Lines, walls and floors use two corners up to 24 blocks apart. Press **R** to rotate a blueprint.

A wireframe previews the placement and turns red when the free cells cannot fit. Blueprints preserve existing blocks where they overlap. Flying allows midair placement. Creative flight moves horizontally at 18 blocks per second; hold Space to rise and Shift to descend.

### Try TNT and undo

Place TNT, aim at it, then press **Q** to light a three-second fuse. A blast clears player-built cubes within 4.5 blocks and triggers nearby TNT. Terrain and scenery stay intact, and there is no player damage.

Press **Z** to undo a build, stop a fuse or restore an entire blast. Press **Y** to redo an undone action. Each brush, blueprint and TNT chain counts as one undo action. History retains 24 actions during the current creative-world visit; it resets when you leave or reload.

Fuses pause in menus and stop when leaving. Completed damage is autosaved, including during longer chains. Restoring blocks lifts the explorer above any overlapping construction. The sound toggle also controls explosion sounds.

## Discover optional landmarks

The first three adventures each have an extra activity. Press **L**, then **E** at the landmark:

- **Meadow Isles:** open the sluice at the waterwheel and waterfall lookout.
- **Crystal Peaks:** play the mint–violet–gold crystal sequence to awaken the aurora.
- **Sunset Sands:** align three sun rings to open the rotating temple gate.

Each landmark awards 12 building blocks once per explorer. Completed discoveries are restored when you return.

## Understand your saves

Up to six explorers can each have a name and a fox, astronaut, dragon or panda avatar. Each keeps separate maths settings, questions, progress, discoveries, inventory, creative hotbar and buildings.

New explorers start with 36 adventure blocks. Correct answers earn three more, and landmarks award 12 once. Adventure islands save up to 600 placed blocks each. Creative materials are unlimited and never consume adventure inventory. Buildings remain when changing worlds or starting a new puzzle round.

Everything is saved in this browser's local storage after changes. There is no account or cross-device sync. Clearing site data removes profiles and progress. A revision check prevents an older open tab from overwriting a newer family save.

Older maths progress is migrated into the first explorer. The original save record is retained, while the family save becomes authoritative. The expanded Grassland Valley keeps the original village's save key, building coordinates and flat central foundations.
